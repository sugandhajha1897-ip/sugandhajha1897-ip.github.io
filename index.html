02 — Profile

A Detail-Oriented Legal Professional

I am a Toronto-based intellectual property professional with more than four years of experience supporting patent and trademark prosecution across Canadian, U.S. and international jurisdictions.

As an IP Coordinator at Smart & Biggar LLP, I manage patent maintenance fees and annuities, review and diarize CIPO correspondence, and keep matter records accurate in Anaqua and iManage.
